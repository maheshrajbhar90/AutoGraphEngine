1. Read and inspect ALL pages of the uploaded PDF, not just the first page.
2. Extract information from every relevant dashboard, card, chart, table, sector board, institutional flow section, and stock opportunity section.
3. Use OCR or visual inspection wherever text extraction is insufficient.
4. Do not rely on previously supplied JSON values as the source of current market data.
5. Every existing data field must be checked against the latest PDF.
6. Update every field with its corresponding value from the latest report.
7. Never assume that a value remains unchanged simply because it appears in the previous JSON.
8. Never use external websites, historical data, market estimates, or general financial knowledge to fill missing PDF values.
9. Do not confuse values belonging to different cards or dashboard sections.
10. Preserve the exact precision and sign of numerical values as displayed in the PDF.

**CRITICAL:** Accuracy is more important than completing the JSON. Never fabricate, estimate, interpolate, or guess a missing value.

If any required value cannot be reliably extracted:
- Reinspect the relevant PDF page.
- Cross-check the same metric in other sections of the report, if available.
- If still unavailable or ambiguous, do not invent a replacement.
- Report the exact missing field and its location.
- Do not claim the JSON is fully verified.

## 2. CARD-WISE DATA MAPPING

Extract the following from the FINAL MARKET BIAS section:

- brand: Keep MARKET INTELLIGENCE.

- bias: Actual final market bias (BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL).

- score: Overall Market Health score.

- sideways: Sideways probability.
- bearish: Bearish continuation probability.
- hook: Generate an engaging, report-supported market hook.

Validation:
- Confidence must come from the confidence metric, not the overall market score.
- The three probabilities must match the report exactly.
- Their total must equal 100%.
- Do not confuse Market Health with Market Confidence.

2. BREADTH

5. RISK

- name

- desc

### CARD 3 — SECTOR PERFORMANCE · 7 DAYS

Extract all sector performance values from the dedicated SECTOR PERFORMANCE section.

Each entry must follow:

["SECTOR_NAME", numerical_7_DAY_PERFORMANCE]

Rules:

- Extract every sector shown in the report.

- Do not use 1-day performance, 3-month relative strength, or 1-month relative strength.

### CARD 4 — INSTITUTIONAL MONEY FLOW

Extract actual FII and DII values from the FII / DII FLOWS section.

Update:

- fii.net
- fii.buy
- fii.sell
- dii.net
- dii.buy
- dii.sell
- accumulation
- distribution
- as_of

Mathematical validation:

FII Net = FII Buy − FII Sell

DII Net = DII Buy − DII Sell

Verify both calculations against the reported net values.

For accumulation and distribution:
- Use the actual percentages from MONEY FLOW BALANCE.
- Do not use institutional heatmap scores.
- Do not substitute stock-level accumulation percentages.
- Accumulation + Distribution must equal 100% if the report presents them as complementary percentages.

Use the actual report date and data status in as_of. Do not assume the previous trading day's date.

### CARD 5 — SECTOR STAGE BOARD

Extract the sector-level relative strength and classifications from the SECTOR STAGE BOARD.

For each sector update:

- name
- m3
- m1
- class
- avoid

Mapping:

- m3 = Actual 3M RS percentage versus NIFTY50.
- m1 = Actual 1M RS percentage versus NIFTY50.
- class = Actual sector classification displayed in the stage board.
- avoid = true only when the report explicitly marks the sector as AVOID.

Important:
- Do not use absolute sector returns.
- Do not use Card 3 seven-day performance.
- Do not use institutional heatmap scores.
- Do not independently classify sectors using your own interpretation.
- Extract every sector displayed in the board.
- Preserve exact numerical signs and decimal precision.

If a sector is marked NEUTRAL / HOLD, do not classify it as WEAKNESS / AVOID.

If it is marked WEAKNESS / AVOID, reflect that classification correctly.

### CARD 6 — STOCK OPPORTUNITY RADAR

Extract stock opportunities from the OPPORTUNITY RADAR section.

For every stock update:

- name
- score
- grade
- risk_cat
- entry
- stop
- risk

Rules:

1. Extract the actual stock symbol.
2. Extract the actual opportunity score.
3. Extract the actual grade (A, B+, B, etc.).
4. Extract the actual risk category (low risk, medium risk, high risk).
5. Extract the actual entry price.
6. Extract the actual stop-loss price.
7. Calculate risk percentage using:

Risk (%) = ((Entry Price − Stop-Loss Price) / Entry Price) × 100

8. Round calculated risk to one decimal place.
9. Cross-check the calculated risk against the displayed risk wherever available.
10. Include all stocks displayed in the Opportunity Radar, not just the first six.
11. Preserve the report's ranking order.
12. Never substitute a stock from the previous JSON if it is absent from the latest report.

If entry, stop-loss, score, grade, or risk category cannot be verified, flag that stock rather than inventing its details.
